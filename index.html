Weekly schedule

Pick which training day lands on each weekday, or choose Off for a rest day with no workout. You can have more than one Off day. Picking a training day that is already placed swaps the two weekdays.

Check-offs, swaps, schedule changes including Off days, your edited sets and reps, and your weights are saved on this device only, in this browser. Every weight you type is remembered per move: the boxes prefill with your last weight for that move, and once you have logged it in an earlier week, the weights from last time show under the move name. Tap History on any move to see every dated session you have logged for it and how its top weight trends over time. Sets and reps are edited per move, per day, so changing one does not change the same move on another day.
